## ✨ What is Crystall?

Crystall is a ground-up rebuild: one translucent overlay with two modes, **Ask** and **Listen**, plus a full dashboard for your chats, meetings, files, prompts, and settings. It's built for the moments where switching to a browser tab would cost you the room: interviews, sales calls, standups, lectures, live debugging.

## 💬 Ask mode

Type a question, dictate it with push-to-talk, or let Crystall see your screen: capture it, drag-select a region, attach files, or turn on **Use image** so every message carries a fresh screenshot. Documents go through built-in OCR and stay in context for follow-up questions. Answers stream in as Markdown, and everything is saved locally where you can search, export, or delete it.

## 🎧 Listen mode

Hit Start and Crystall transcribes your mic and system audio live, with speaker labels and language selection. Automatic responses fire when someone asks a question, after every pause, or only when you tap Suggest. Smart follow-up chips appear under each answer, generated from the actual conversation. Every session is saved as a meeting with its full transcript.
